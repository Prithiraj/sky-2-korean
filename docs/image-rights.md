# Image Rights & Production Notes

## Production status

The current deployed site uses **real Creative Commons photography** of Korean cuisine. These images are legally reusable under their respective licenses when attribution requirements are followed, but they are **not photographs of The Sky 2 Korean Restaurant**.

They are intentionally labelled in the UI and should be replaced with restaurant-owned originals for the strongest commercial launch.

## Business-specific reference imagery

During research, actual Sky 2 photography was visible on Google/restaurant listing and review platforms. Those images are useful for art direction, but were **not copied into the deployed site** because public visibility does not automatically grant commercial reuse rights.

Before replacing the Commons images, request owner-controlled originals for:

1. Tabletop grill with food actively cooking.
2. Full table with grill and banchan.
3. Signature BBQ close-up.
4. Kimchi or other house soup.
5. Bibimbap / rice dish if currently offered.
6. Dining room wide angle.
7. Restaurant entrance / signage.
8. Team or owner portrait.
9. Group/private table if promoted.
10. Soju / makgeolli setting if the restaurant wants alcohol featured.

## Replacement rule

When owner images arrive:

- Preserve meaningful alt text based on the real contents of each replacement image.
- Prefer AVIF/WebP exports with responsive widths.
- Remove the illustrative-photo disclaimers from the public UI.
- Update Open Graph image and JSON-LD `image` property.
- Keep written proof of commercial web usage rights for every production image.
