The experience

Part dinner, part ritual.

Sky 2 is built around the shared-table rhythm of Korean dining: a hot grill in the middle, banchan around the edges, and dishes designed to be passed, tasted and talked over.

Public listings and reviews consistently describe the choice to grill at the table or have the kitchen prepare the BBQ, alongside soups, rice dishes and Korean drinks.

Why Sky 2

A Korean grill hiding in plain sight.

The location is part of the character: walk into Golden Tulip Media Hotel in Barsha Heights, head to the first floor, and the experience shifts from hotel corridor to shared Korean table.

The strongest reason to choose Sky 2 is not novelty or spectacle. It is the combination of tabletop cooking, varied side dishes, soups and a group-friendly meal in an accessible Tecom address.
